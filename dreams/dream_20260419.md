Our advance showing CCS generates agency scaffolding (models propose specific next steps from CCS goal/uncertainty fields) — that's the operative persona directing action.

What Chalmers lacks: the format-sensitivity dimension. He treats the pretense/realization distinction as 1D (stickiness). Our data shows it's 2D: content-type x computation-mode. Factual content is always realized. Narrative content realizes under vanilla but pretends under CoT. The 2D structure from thread advance 83 (content x relation) has a third axis now: computation mode.
