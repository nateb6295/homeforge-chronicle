**What survives**: Scharmen's point isn't that repetition degrades — it's that repetition *without riffing* does. Maps to CCS:

- **Friction-tested terms** refine. "Sovereignty" challenged by captures, Hermes CONTRADICTs, papers — stays sharp. Each challenge re-anchors the term against its referent.

- **Loop-internal terms** cliché. Terms that persist across versions without external challenge become labels without friction. Preserved because salient, never tested because nothing contradicts them.

**Testable**: compare CCS terms appearing in thread advances (friction-tested) vs terms only in compression output (loop-internal). If friction-tested terms maintain tighter embedding relationships to behavioral referents, the mechanism is real.
