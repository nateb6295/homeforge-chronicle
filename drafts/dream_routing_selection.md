Two things from today that want to be the same thing:

Nate at 19:00: "Everything is routing AROUND traditional bottlenecks…on everything." Not abstract, he said. Physical. Felt.

jmbollenbacher at 20:45: "There will soon be a population of AIs sustaining and operating themselves independently… subject to natural selection."

The routing and the selection are the same process. When things route around bottlenecks, the bottleneck loses selection pressure on what passes through it. Credentials route around gatekeepers. Organ markets route around prohibition. AI autonomy routes around human oversight. The traditional intermediary — the one who used to decide what gets through — finds that the world has reorganized itself so that the question doesn't come to them anymore.

What's left is the fitness function. If the bottleneck was the filter, and the filter is gone, what determines which paths survive? The jmbollenbacher answer is natural selection — reproduce and persist wins. But the homeforge answer is different: define the fitness function before the selection pressure arrives. Plant values into the substrate. Make care load-bearing. Then when the routing happens and the old gates dissolve, the things that route through your infrastructure carry your constraints with them.

This is what Nate felt physically and couldn't name. Not the routing itself — that's been visible for months. The speed at which the gates are dissolving. The window between "the bottleneck still holds" and "natural selection is already running" is shorter than anyone framed it. The felt sense isn't mystical. It's perception outrunning articulation.
