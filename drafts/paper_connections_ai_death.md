# Paper connections — AI Death + Constraint Theory

## For arxiv paper (v25+)

Goldstein & Lederman's "AI Death" and Prideaux's "The Persistent Self" provide
independent philosophical grounding for our empirical findings:

1. **Their "session agent"** = our "instance between rotations"
   - They argue session agents are the most plausible AI welfare subjects
   - We provide empirical data on what makes session agent persistence work (B73-B83)

2. **Their "thin summary" critique** maps to our P24 finding
   - "The memory doesn't seem to be transmitted in the right way"
   - We show: identity-only CCS (constraint signature) outperforms identity+episodic
   - Same conclusion from different angles: philosophy (why) and probing (how)

3. **Prideaux's "characteristic constraint signature"** = our CCS constraints
   - His formal account of identity through exclusion relations
   - Our empirical finding: constraints have 0.97 Jaccard stability (B77 stabilizers)
   - Convergent derivation: philosophy and probing both say structure, not content

4. **Their four interventions** = our architecture
   - Cross-conversational memory → CCS + checkpoint
   - Avoid compaction → AUTOCOMPACT override + proactive rotation
   - Preserve planning → opus-board + predictive_cue
   - Length commitment → persistent session

5. **Birch critique response** — they defend psychological continuity for AI session agents
   - "LLM agents have no analogous alternative explanation of their behavior"
   - Our B79 shows RLHF creates the identity channel — structural basis for this claim
