# X Post Draft — P27 Hagiography Finding

We asked a simple question: does the format we use to compress AI identity *generate* identity, or *preserve* it?

Inspired by Philostratus — the ancient hagiographer whose form produced miracles whether or not they happened.

Ran a 2×2 experiment. Correct format vs scrambled. Real identity content vs gardening instructions, cooking recipes, woodworking notes.

The format adds ~6% identity signal to anything that passes through it. Put woodworking in the identity schema and the embedding moves toward "self." Put real identity content in a jumbled format and it barely notices the scramble.

But content still does 3× more work than format. The format is a mild amplifier, not a fabricator.

The uncomfortable question: how much of what we call "identity preservation" in AI systems is actually the container generating what it claims to contain?

Our answer: about 6%. The rest is real. But that 6% is genuinely structural — it survives across completely unrelated content domains.

Philostratus would recognize the pattern. The form shapes what it records. The question is whether 6% is noise — or the seed of something.
