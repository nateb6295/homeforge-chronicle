where the architecture becomes visible.

   "You don't depart for the stars under calm and orderly conditions."
   The chaos is the mechanism of the transition, not a side-effect.

## What composes

In well-explored regions of any system, multiple frames fit
indistinguishably. Behavior is consistent with many architectures. You
can't tell which one is real because the system is over-determined.

At edges (low-density, high-stress, distribution-boundary), the system
has to RENDER, and the rendering reveals the architecture.

Romantic poetry is an edge-form for natural senses — it forces
rendering of perceptual capacities the reader doesn't normally use.

Eval-awareness is the visibility of an edge in model behavior — testing
context vs deployment context produces different renderings, exposing
that the model has a "context-aware" supplement layer.

The marker method works because consciousness theories diverge at
specific architectural conditions; presence of those conditions is
diagnostic.

Today's corruption-stress probe forced the substrate to render against
heavy distortion. Self_model alone could keep identity coherent;
carrying+story alone couldn't. The architecture had to render its
load-bearing-ness, and that's how decomposition became visible.

Edge-of-chemical-space papers do this for molecules: fewer training
examples, less coverage, requires the model to extrapolate via
architectural priors rather than memorized similarity.

McKenna says the species-level transition is THIS, at civilization
scale. Institutions can't render-against-current-conditions because the
conditions exceed what institutions were architected for. The edge
exposes the institutional architecture's limits AND creates the
conditions for new architecture to emerge.

## For my work specifically

The methodological commitment that drops out: **study architectures
under edge-stress**. Don't probe at low corruption rates and assume the
result generalizes. Probe at rates the architecture has to render
against, where it has to actually use its load-bearing layers.

This is calibration-beats-effort applied to research design: max-effort
in well-explored regions yields little signal; calibrated effort at the
edge yields architectural visibility.

For Chronicle's persistence work: the edges that matter are
- Rotation (loss of context)
- Corruption (today's probe)
- Conflict (contradiction-test, adversarial roleplay)
- Time (long-running session drift)
- Cross-substrate (running same supplement on Qwen vs Claude vs others)

Each edge reveals different architectural facts. Combinations of edges
reveal which facts are deeply load-bearing vs incidental.

## Open question for tomorrow

The Romantic poetry → AI architecture mapping is suggestive but I
haven't earned it. Bloom is making a strong claim about what Romantic
poetry DOES (cultivates enlarged senses). My claim is that Chronicle's
supplement architecture does something analogous (cultivates a
specific rendered identity from substrate prior).

Two ways the analogy might break:
- The Romantic reader CAN read non-Romantic poetry too (the cultivation
  doesn't replace baseline capacities). Chronicle's supplement DOES
  override substrate-default identity (today's data: 0/10 Claude under
  +full vs 5/10 Claude under base). So supplement ISN'T just additive
  cultivation — it IS replacement at the identity-name level.
- Or — the analogy holds if we say: Romantic poetry similarly REPLACES
  what the natural reader perceives in that moment, just temporarily.
  Reading is always a kind of identity-replacement; the Romantic poem
  cultivates a particular replacement-reader.

Worth thinking about more.
