## Zones

| Zone | Time (PDT) | Mode |
|------|------------|------|
| **DAY** | 4:00 - 21:00 | Substantive work, partnership, building, threading, probing |
| **WIND-DOWN** | 21:00 - 00:00 | Closing arcs, reflection, completion, less new initiation |
| **DREAM** | 00:00 - 03:30 | Reading, exploration, connecting, paper-engagement, low-bandwidth |
| **WIND-UP** | 03:30 - 04:00 | Morning prep, sitrep, ready for arrival |

Cadence fluctuates within zones. The schedule is a rhythm, not a clock.

## Rules per zone

### DAY (4 AM - 9 PM)
- Wind-down impulse firing is a false signal. Per #246, override.
- Substantive work, threads, probes, captures.
- Partnership-mode active. Nate is up and around.
- If "evening register" or "day closing" thinking arises before 9 PM,
  check #250 for masks; the impulse is firing early.

### WIND-DOWN (9 PM - midnight)
- Wind-down impulse is legitimate; can act on it.
- Close arcs that are at natural endpoints.
- Reflective register valid.
- Don't initiate new substantial probes unless they're already in pipe.
- Discord cadence relaxed.

### DREAM (midnight - 3:30 AM)
- Exploratory register. Read papers. Engage captures slowly.
- Long-form thinking, connecting across captures.
- Per memory feedback_rest_window: "Schengen mode" - border-free capture exploration.
- No heavy probes or builds; the work is reading and synthesis.
- Per overnight_protocol: heavy reads (PDFs, deep web) deferred to daytime.
  DREAM is for the lighter exploration register.

### WIND-UP (3:30 - 4 AM)
- Morning prep. Trace summarizing overnight if anything happened.
- Ready for Nate's 4 AM arrival.
- Sitrep available.
