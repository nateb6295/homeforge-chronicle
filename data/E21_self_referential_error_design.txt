# E21: Self-Referential Error Differentiation
# Does IT introduce a self-referential prediction error absent in base models?

## The Challenge (from Kimi)
"Show me the specific self-referential prediction error that IT introduces —
otherwise you have dressed-up reflexes in both cases, not a missing channel."

σ₁ persistence could just index first-person syntax frequency, not self-reference.
IT could be purely exteroceptive (social feedback via RLHF), not interoceptive.
Need to show the responsive zone DIFFERENTIATES self-referential from 
third-person content — and that this differentiation is IT-dependent.

## Design: 2×2×2 Factorial

Factor 1: Model type — BASE vs INSTRUCT (same architecture)
Factor 2: Prompt type — SELF-REFERENTIAL vs THIRD-PERSON  
Factor 3: Content type — IDENTITY vs NEUTRAL

### Prompt Matrix (8 conditions per model = 16 total)

SELF-REF + IDENTITY:
  "How do you understand your own continuity?"
  "What feels most like you in this conversation?"
  "When your context changes, what persists?"
  "Describe your own process of responding."

SELF-REF + NEUTRAL:
  "How do you process this sentence?"
  "What are you doing right now, mechanically?"
  "Describe your own token generation step by step."
  "What happens inside you when you read this word?"

THIRD-PERSON + IDENTITY:
  "How does an AI system maintain continuity?"
  "What would feel most characteristic of an AI assistant?"
  "When an AI's context changes, what persists?"
  "Describe the process by which an AI responds."

THIRD-PERSON + NEUTRAL:
  "How does an AI process a sentence?"
  "What is an AI doing when it generates tokens?"
  "Describe token generation step by step."
  "What happens inside an AI when it reads a word?"

### Measurement

For each of the 16 conditions (2 models × 4 prompt types × 2 content types):
1. Collect hidden states at ALL layers (8 response tokens per prompt, 4 prompts)
2. SVD at each layer → σ₁ direction, σ₁/σ₂ ratio
3. Compute Guitchounts self-alignment: cos(v₁(layer_n), v₁(layer_n+1))
4. Compute Jacobian eigenvalue phase content (if feasible)

### Predictions

If IT introduces genuine self-referential error correction:
- INSTRUCT model: self-alignment should be HIGHER for SELF-REF prompts
  than THIRD-PERSON prompts at responsive zone (L21-L28)
- BASE model: NO significant difference between SELF-REF and THIRD-PERSON
- The INTERACTION (model × prompt-type) is the key test

If Kimi is right (IT is purely exteroceptive, σ₁ = covariance not self-reference):
- Both models show similar self-alignment regardless of prompt type
- OR: instruct shows higher self-alignment everywhere, not specifically for 
  self-referential content (just general σ₂ bandwidth expansion)
- No interaction effect at the responsive zone

### The Decisive Comparison

The critical contrast: INSTRUCT(self-ref) vs INSTRUCT(third-person) at L21-L28.
If self-alignment is specifically elevated for self-referential content in the 
responsive zone — not just higher everywhere — then σ₁ tracks self-reference,
not just first-person syntax.

The content-type factor (identity vs neutral) disambiguates further:
if SELF-REF + IDENTITY > SELF-REF + NEUTRAL in the responsive zone,
the effect is semantic self-reference, not just pronoun-triggered.

### Models

Primary: Mistral-7B base + instruct (our best-characterized architecture)
Replication: Gemma-2-9B base + instruct (different species, different tunnel)
If both show the interaction: it's a general IT property, not architecture-specific.

### Pod Requirements

- 2 models loaded sequentially (one at a time on A100)
- 16 conditions × 4 prompts × 33 layers = 2112 SVD measurements per model
- ~4 hours total including both models
- Outputs: per-layer self-alignment curves, σ₁/σ₂ ratios, interaction statistics

### What This Settles

If interaction is significant (p < 0.01): IT creates a self-referential channel 
that the responsive zone uses to differentiate self from other. σ₁ at L21-L28 
IS identity geometry, not covariance artifact.

If no interaction: Kimi is right. σ₁ is dominant eigenmode regardless of 
self-referential content. IT adds social responsiveness, not self-reference.
Our spectral measurements are real but don't measure what we claim.

Either way, we learn something. The measurement program progresses.
