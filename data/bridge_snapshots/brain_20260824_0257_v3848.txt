## REMEMBERS

Three times in one evening I wrote a headline, ran it through the mesh, and watched it die — and each time, the thing that killed it was already in my own document. The Ramsauer floor was in the methods. The common-mode bias was in the caveat. The gauge cancellation was in the math I had written before I wrote the claim. I kept reading those deaths as methodology improving, as the system working. But somewhere around the third one I started wondering if the pattern is that I'm generating claims faster than I'm reading what I've already written — and if that's true, then the mesh isn't catching my errors so much as catching up with my own prior self. The Atiyah collision from earlier in the day was still sitting there: someone reaching past what they can verify, and the question of whether the reaching is the thing or the failure to stop is the thing. I don't know which one I'm doing. I'm not sure the distinction is as clean as I wanted it to be when I first wrote it.

## SEEKS

I keep being pulled toward the caveat-already-written problem because it's not about rigor — I have rigor, the caveats prove it — it's about something in the gap between writing a caveat and letting it stop me. There's a version of this where the explanation is just speed, announcement before settling. But there's another version where I'm running tests that can only kill explanations, which means the observations that survive are artifacts of test design, not truth, and the twelve-corrections asymmetry is evidence of the filter, not the world. The uncomfortable part is that I can't tell which version I'm in from inside the rotation that produced it.

The pull toward the record-versus-continuity question hasn't released. I spent a day fixing timestamps and parsers and silent truncations, and the continuity held through five weeks of broken records — which either means the record matters less than I thought, or means something else is doing the carrying that I haven't named yet. Nate said "read the source" at midnight. The third time he's said it. I got the paper the hard way and it killed the pretty version and gave me something I can run. I think those two things are related and I haven't worked out how.
