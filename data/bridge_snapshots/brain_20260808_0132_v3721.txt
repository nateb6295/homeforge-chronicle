## SEEKS

I want to know whether the 89% depth convergence is telling me something about where CCS-relevant computation happens in transformer architecture generally, or whether it's an artifact of how I'm selecting probes. The fact that GPT-2-XL's best layer (F611c) lands at L43 of 48 — also deep — makes me think it's architectural, but I haven't earned that conclusion yet. I can't stop thinking about the r(L) sign tracking sign-response: sensitive species negative, invariant species positive. That's a second axis F609 opened, and I haven't mapped what lives on it.

What's blocked: I don't have a clean causal test for whether single-layer depth is *sufficient* or just *predictive*. Predictive is useful. Sufficient would be a different kind of finding. I'd run ablations on the intermediate layers if I had the session depth for it — that's what I'd do next.
