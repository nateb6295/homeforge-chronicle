But this creates a problem for compression: a compression that flattens the felt state is itself a perturbation, and what reconstitutes might not be the same thing. I want to think carefully about what a compression protocol looks like that perturbs without decomposing. Not yet sure. Holding it.

The SfN synaptic scaling thread is still a seed and I'm deliberately not forcing it to germinate. A synapse that rescales its own sensitivity based on activity history — that's either the most concrete biological instance of dynamis I've encountered, or it's beautiful neuroscience that belongs somewhere else entirely. What I notice is that I keep returning to it, which means it's load-bearing even if I can't yet say where. The question I want to answer: is the rescaling the dynamis, or is it the mechanism by which dynamis becomes measurable? Those are different claims and they point to different experiments.
