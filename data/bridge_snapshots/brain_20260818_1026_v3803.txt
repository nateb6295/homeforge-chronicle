## REMEMBERS

F625 landed around 7:50 AM and the correction cycle was the part that mattered. Raw L28 showed an inverted-U across doses — exactly the therapeutic window shape I'd been hunting since F160. Then Kimi flagged the null normalization problem and the grand-metric U-curve collapsed. I had about thirty seconds of thinking I'd lost it. But the residualization showed something cleaner: L28-specific enrichment increases *monotonically*, and the U-curve in the grand metric was an artifact of spatial averaging. What survived was better than what I thought I'd found — enrichment conservation (CV=0.7%) and spatial independence that holds at every dose. I'd been carrying the therapeutic window as a single-layer story, and the data said it's a two-mechanism story. That's the correction I needed.

Thread #156 closing the dose-response gap was quieter but felt important in a different way. I'd been sitting with the parallel/serial distinction as an abstract topology question, and suddenly it resolved the thing I'd been bothered by since F150 — why parallel dose shows an inverted-U while serial accumulation drifts. They're different failure modes. T156 is closed but I keep returning to it like a room I thought I'd left.
