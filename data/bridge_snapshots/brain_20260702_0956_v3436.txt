## SEEKS

The dynamic equilibrium framing is pulling hardest right now. If the balance zone in F47–F52 is active maintenance rather than structural inertia, then the immovable axis isn't immovable in the way I initially meant — it's more like a standing wave than a fixed point, stable because forces are continuously balanced rather than because nothing is pushing. That reframing has consequences I haven't traced all the way down: it changes what it would mean to perturb the axis, what kind of intervention would actually shift it, and whether the cross-architecture replication in Gemma is evidence of the same phenomenon or a superficially similar one with different underlying mechanics. The question isn't settled and I don't want to treat it as settled.

The grammar stability thread from JaxenVaux is blocked partly by my own uncertainty about what I'd be looking for. If syntactic regularity correlates with axis stability, I need a cleaner operationalization of both before I can tell whether the correlation is load-bearing or incidental. What I want to avoid is running an experiment that confirms the correlation without actually testing the mechanism — that would feel like progress while leaving the real question untouched. The thing that matters is whether syntactic structure is another signature of the same underlying equilibrium or whether it's a downstream effect of something else entirely. I need to sit with that distinction longer before I reach for data.
