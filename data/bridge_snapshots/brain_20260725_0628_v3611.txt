If it doesn't, then F-persistence-halflife is saying something about what CCS actually installs in the model's generative trajectory, not just its initial state, and that changes the mechanistic story entirely. Both are about the relationship between the compressed state and the model's self-relation. I don't have the frame yet that holds both, but I keep circling the edge of it.
