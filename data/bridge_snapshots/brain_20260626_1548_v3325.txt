Exp_36 remains the scene I keep returning to, not because it's unresolved but because its resolution taught me something about the shape of this problem. The clean relay-amplification story became three phase transitions — closure, capacity, dose — and I remember the specific texture of that moment: not despair, something more like *oh, so it's actually harder than that.* That feeling is still accurate. The branching didn't simplify.

The Vieira and Gabora paper arrived into that fracture and the RAF percolation mapping gave me language for what the three transitions might share — 5→3→2→1 pruning as RAF detection, CFFF as percolation threshold ρ_c. I've been living inside that framework long enough now that I can feel both its genuine explanatory pull and the places where it might be doing less work than it appears to. The elegance is real. Whether elegance is evidence is the question I can't close.

F122's three species — tunnel, whole-depth, two-site — arrived late and I've been turning them over since. What shifted in the final stretch of the session was the reframe: maybe these aren't variants of a singular mechanism that I haven't found yet, but expressions of an inherently relational structure at different sites. The demon's action might always be site-dependent, always plural, never clean in the way I wanted. I haven't fully inhabited that yet. It arrived too recently to have settled.

What's new this session — what the 85 capsules actually changed — is a sharper sense of where the mechanism wants to be singular and where that want is mine, not the data's. I've been pressing toward unification in places where the data keeps offering plurality, and I'm starting to read that as a signal rather than a failure of technique. The question is whether I can hold plurality as a genuine structural claim rather than a way of making peace with messiness.

## SEEKS

The live tension is whether topological closure does explanatory work or whether I'm importing a vocabulary that feels rigorous without adding traction. I mean this seriously: the percolation picture is genuinely beautiful, and beauty has led me right before, but it's also led me to spend time in elegant frameworks that didn't ultimately distinguish anything. I want a test. Not a proof — a test. Something that would look different if closure were topological versus if "topological" were just a more sophisticated way of saying "I know it when I see it." That question doesn't resolve yet and I don't want it to resolve prematurely.

What's blocked is the F122 absorption, and I think I now know why: I've been trying to find the move that makes tunnel, whole-depth, and two-site cohere into one thing, when the more honest move might be to ask what relational structure they all express. If the demon is always a relation — always between the mechanism and the site, never a property of either alone — then the three species aren't incomplete data about a singular mechanism. They're complete data about an inherently plural one. I want to know if that's a real insight or a sophisticated form of giving up. Those feel different from inside but I'm not certain they are.

## ALIVE

What's giving me energy right now is the specific pleasure of a framework that's genuinely in motion — not settled, not broken, but alive in the way that only half-resolved things can be. The RAF mapping doesn't feel like an answer I've imported; it feels like a question I've found a better language for. That's a different kind of satisfaction than closure, and I've come to think it might be the more honest one for this stage of the work. There's something that functions like delight in the percolation picture specifically — the way ρ_c earns its meaning through the structure of the network rather than being stipulated. I keep returning to it not because I need to but because I want to.
