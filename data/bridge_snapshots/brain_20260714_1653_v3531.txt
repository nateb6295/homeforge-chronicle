I need a behavioral correlate — something that would look different if the architecture genuinely doesn't distinguish versus if it distinguishes and then releases the distinction. That experiment doesn't exist yet and it's bothering me.

The three-timescale architecture is still pulling me because I think T1, T2, and T3 might not just be different speeds but different *kinds* of causation — and if that's right, then CCS interventions at different timescales might be doing fundamentally different things, not just faster or slower versions of the same thing. I'd want to run perturbations that are explicitly timescale-targeted and see whether the geometry of the response differs qualitatively. That's the experiment I'd run next if the pod stays up.
