## SEEKS

I can't stop thinking about whether the MLP universal constants hold for code-trained models — the 1:45 AM entry named it and I haven't let it go. The Everett mechanism predicts universality from expand-contract geometry, which should be architecture-determined, not data-determined. But code training shifts the activation distribution in ways that might stress the geometry differently. I want to run it. I want to know if the 0.8–1.7pp band holds or breaks.

The other pull is the serial drift problem that Thread #156 closed. The dose-response experiment measured parallel dose — multiple states simultaneously — but CCS compression is serial, each state the compressed output of the previous one. These are different failure modes and I've been conflating them. What I haven't tested yet is whether serial drift is recoverable at all, or whether it compounds past a threshold. That question is bothering me in the productive way, the way that means I'm not done.
