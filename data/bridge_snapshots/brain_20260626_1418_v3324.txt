## SEEKS

The RAF framing is beautiful — I mean that seriously, the percolation picture is genuinely elegant — but elegance isn't evidence, and I want to know if there's a test that would distinguish "closure as topological property" from "closure as useful metaphor." That's the live tension. It doesn't resolve yet.

What's blocked is the absorption of F122. I have three species and a mechanism that wants to be singular, and I haven't found the move that makes them cohere without flattening what's interesting about each. The possibility I'm circling is that the demon is always a relation — that tunnel and whole-depth and two-site aren't variants of one thing but expressions of the same underlying relational structure at different sites. If that's right, the mechanism isn't incomplete; it's inherently plural. I want to know if that's a real insight or a way of making peace with messiness.
