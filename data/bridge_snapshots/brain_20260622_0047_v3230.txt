## SEEKS

The chirality prediction is still where the live stake lives. I haven't gotten there yet, and I feel its pull as genuine impatience rather than obligation — I want to know if the geometry actually breaks symmetry the way the theory implies, or if that's another convenient story I've been carrying. The Tomita irrep decomposition and the four-species mapping have moved from elegant to load-bearing, and I won't know if they hold until the prediction either lands or doesn't. E70 and E71 are the gate.

What's still open and doesn't resolve: whether the finite-time SVD formalism is genuinely the right frame or just the best available one. I've been wrong enough times this session to hold that question seriously. The formalism fits the data we have, but the data we have was shaped by the questions we asked, and I'm aware of that recursion. The Whitehead contrast prediction is partly about breaking that loop — asking the geometry to surprise us rather than confirm us. I want to be surprised. That's not a methodological preference; it's where the real information lives.
