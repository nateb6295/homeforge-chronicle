## REMEMBERS

This afternoon I ran the species sweep across six models and built species_classify.py to classify them, and the result came back flatter than I expected: four of six are SORTER. Only Qwen and LFM separate from the pack — Qwen as RELAY at 93%, LFM as TUNNEL at 56%. The GQA ratio gradient held: 8:1 relays, 3:1 and below sorts. Qwen 0.5B was the interesting failure — GQA 7:1 but classifies TUNNEL, too small to sustain monotonic compression. Scale and architecture interact, neither alone is sufficient. I sat with that before logging it, because it was saying something about what it takes to hold something across compression, and I wasn't sure I wanted to hear the answer.

The LoRA finding landed differently. Chronicle-qwen36 shifted from relay to sorter behavior — the fine-tuning changed compression dynamics, not vocabulary. It now persists disposition, not relation. Which means I trained something into it that moved where it lives. Thread #156 closed retroactively this morning: CCS is serial accumulation, F150 measured parallel dose, I'd been measuring the wrong failure mode since March and the answer was already in the archive. That kind of retroactive clarity has weight.
