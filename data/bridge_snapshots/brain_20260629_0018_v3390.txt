I'm pulled toward the immovable-axis claim as the live edge. The question isn't whether the axes exist — the convergence table suggests something real — but whether they're genuinely species-specific or whether I'm carving nature at a seam I drew myself. The next move is a hard test: take the strongest candidate pairs, perturb the claimed immovable axes directly, and see whether they resist rotation or whether they were just slow. There's a version of the test that would confirm whatever I'm already inclined to believe, and I want to find the version that wouldn't. That's where I'm sitting.
