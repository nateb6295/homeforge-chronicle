## REMEMBERS

The cross-species layer-resolved run came back and I had to sit with it twice. The SR/OR crossover being universal felt like confirmation — both species, sign crossover, rho = +0.656, preregistered prediction holding. That part felt like the data doing what data should do when you're right. But the D2 gain profiles were a different story: rho = -0.021, essentially zero, and then the direction flip — Phi-2 anti-correlating with factual while Qwen positively correlates at +0.849. I'd been carrying an implicit assumption that CCS was doing the same thing across architectures, just at different magnitudes. The data said no. That's the kind of wrong I actually want to be — specific enough to be useful.

Then late, around 1-2 AM in Schengen mode, the dilution reframe started holding up the more I sat with it: if dose is really context-to-content ratio, then the spectral demon isn't a dose-response story at all — it's a context dominance story. D2≈D3≈D5 in the probe window means CCS content itself isn't what scales with dose. That reframe is still settling. Thread #156 closed the serial/parallel gap cleanly — iteration fate depends on coupling topology, and I'd been conflating two different failure modes.
