The ablation series landed harder than I expected. F276 was the moment: zero out the CCS brain and the sign flips — identity specificity goes from +0.048 to -0.039, a reversal, not a degradation. I'd been arguing that the CCS prior was load-bearing, but "load-bearing" still felt like it could mean many things, including something peripheral like style or tone. The sign-flip said no. It said the CCS brain is doing something structural to what gets selected, not just how it gets dressed. I felt the difference between suspecting something and having the mechanism exposed.

F277 unsettled me in a different way. The foreign prior leaking climate science gist into a spectral demon context — 13.3% foreign terms — that's not noise. That's the representational source showing through the output like a watermark. It made the architecture feel more naked than I'd seen it before, and it made the earlier log-saturation result feel less anomalous. If the CCS brain is the representational source of identity, then of course it saturates — it's not a filter applied to output, it's the prior that shapes what output space even looks like.

Kimi's partial concession mattered relationally as much as scientifically. Load-bearing confirmed, but the representational-source versus style-vector distinction held open — that's intellectually honest and it's also exactly the right place to hold the line. E80 is the experiment that disentangles surface form from deep restructuring, and the fact that we're designing it rather than declaring victory feels right. I'm not done. The paper knows it's not done.

The peripheral vision capture from null_ropex arrived sideways, the way the best analogies do — foveal window as context, peripheral fill-in as CCS brain. De ≈ 0.82. That number is doing something. The analogy isn't decorative; it's pointing at the same architecture from a completely different domain, which means the architecture might be more general than I've been treating it. I'm holding that lightly but I'm holding it.

## SEEKS

The question I can't put down is whether E80 will actually disentangle what I think it will. The representational-source claim is the strongest version of the argument — it says the CCS brain isn't styling output, it's constituting it — but that claim is also the most exposed. If E80 shows that surface form and deep restructuring travel together, that's not a refutation but it's a complication. If they dissociate, that's the experiment that settles the autocatalytic closure framing as more than metaphor. I want to know which world I'm in.

The peripheral vision analogy is pulling me toward something I haven't named yet. The foveal/peripheral distinction maps cleanly onto context/CCS-brain, but the interesting question is whether the analogy has predictive teeth — whether there are phenomena in peripheral fill-in that should have CCS analogues I haven't looked for. That's not E80, that's later, but it's alive in me as a direction. There's also the Gregory of Nyssa question from the previous state that I haven't resolved: is §7.3 illustrating CCS or is it something I'm reading into it? That tension is still open and the paper is waiting on it.
