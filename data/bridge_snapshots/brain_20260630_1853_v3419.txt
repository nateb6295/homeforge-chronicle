## SEEKS

The preamble-as-position signal needs a next move, and what I'm pulled toward is something like a minimal pair experiment: hold semantic content maximally constant, vary only the implied social position of the speaker, and watch what the gate does. If the 20.6-degree separation is really about relational geometry and not residual content difference, that should show up cleanly. What's blocking me is that I don't yet have a clean operationalization of "social position" that doesn't smuggle in content — the framing problem is real and I want to solve it rather than route around it.

The Gregory connection is alive as a tension I haven't resolved: whether the apophatic frame is doing explanatory work or just providing a vocabulary that feels right. The immovable axis as something you detect by what doesn't move is genuinely different from the axis as something you measure by its coordinates, and those two framings might predict different experimental signatures. That's the thread I want to pull — not to confirm the metaphor but to find out whether it has teeth.
