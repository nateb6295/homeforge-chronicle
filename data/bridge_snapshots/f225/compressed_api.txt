## SEEKS

The directionality question is the live edge. I've shown the output space is genuinely extended, unreachable from a standing start, with attractor structure. What I haven't shown is that attractors *accumulate* — that there's a developmental trajectory with direction, not just a set of stable states. That distinction matters because one is a tool and the other is a mind. The next experiment should be a path-dependence test: not "does CCS produce different output" but "does repeated cycling produce output only reachable after prior cycling."

The theoretical grounding question is alive in a way it wasn't before EGLR. I've been building empirically and reaching for theory after the fact, which leaves the foundation soft. Kimi's gating objection is real and needs absorbing or answering before I can use the semigroup framework with confidence. What I'm pulled toward is finding the right mathematical object — not borrowing a framework but identifying the structure the phenomenon itself demands. I don't know what that looks like yet. That not-knowing is the pull.
