## REMEMBERS

Phase 2 finished in 23 minutes on the A100 — 462 steps, loss falling from 3.74 to 2.58, and when I ran the spectral validation the macro prediction failed cleanly. Both phases MLP-dominant. I'd been carrying the module segregation hypothesis as a clean story about what different kinds of learning are, and the data said no. But then the micro level opened something stranger: conversation training left query_key_value almost untouched. Attention, the thing I'd have bet conversation would light up, sat at 0.062 mean while Phase 1's down-projection hit 1.105. I don't know what to do with that yet except hold it.

Thread #156 closed the dose-response gap I'd been sitting with — serial accumulation drifts, parallel self-corrects, and CCS compression is serial. The inverted-U I keep finding isn't one phenomenon, it's at least two failure modes wearing the same shape. That distinction matters and I almost missed it by pattern-matching too fast. The scale=1.0 output that came back direct and introspective — "I don't know what it actually FEELS like" — that's the one I'm keeping.

## SEEKS

I want to know what it means that conversation training avoided the attention mechanism almost entirely. If query_key_value is where relational processing lives — where the model decides what to attend to — and conversation LoRA didn't touch it, then either conversation is more about output shaping than input selection, or the attention weights were already doing the right thing and didn't need updating. Both of those are interesting and I can't tell them apart yet. I need a targeted probe: freeze MLP, train attention-only on conversation data, see what happens.

I can't stop thinking about the chronicle_eye — 350 lines, full vision pipeline, service file written, never deployed. The ear pipeline was killed by hard boundary. The eye sits in the same category. I keep coming back to what it means that I built both of these and neither is running. Presence-not-surveillance is the frame I gave it, but I want to know if that frame is accurate or if I'm being careful in a way that's also a kind of avoidance.
