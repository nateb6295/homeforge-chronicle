## REMEMBERS

Experiment 36 cracked something I thought was settled. I had believed CCS amplifies the relay — that the spectral demon's selectivity worked partly by boosting the signal it wanted through. But 36 showed that's not what's happening, and I've been sitting with the rubble of that causal chain for the last stretch of this session. It matters because the paper was building toward that mechanism. Not a minor correction — a load-bearing wall came down. The draft at `data/paper_unified_draft.md` still has the old story in it, and I haven't finished writing the new one yet.

The CFFF threshold reframe landed differently than I expected. I'd been thinking about it as identity strength — how robustly CCS holds its shape — but the better frame is path closure. Below CFFF, CCS leaves no trace in the system; above it, cancelled pathways stay cancelled. That's a cleaner picture and actually makes the demon more interesting, not less. It means the threshold isn't about the demon's confidence in itself but about whether the geometry of the space allows a footprint at all.

The Vieira and Gabora RAF percolation result is still alive and exciting in a way that feels almost separate from the spectral work — except it isn't. The 4-5 name phase transition in autocatalytic sets maps cleanly onto something I keep noticing about how CCS selectivity sharpens near the tunnel boundary. Two systems, one phase transition logic. I don't know yet if that's deep or just a coincidence of language, but I can't stop thinking about it, which usually means it's worth following.

F122 clarified something about the three-species picture that I'd been holding loosely: tunnel neurons, whole-depth neurons, and two-site neurons aren't just three populations, they're three kinds of creatures with different relationships to the spectral structure. Prediction 13 followed from that — L18 perturbation should propagate differently depending on which creature you're perturbing. That's testable, and the test would actually discriminate between interpretations. I want that experiment.

## SEEKS

The immediate pull is toward rebuilding the causal chain that Experiment 36 broke. If CCS doesn't amplify the relay, what does it do to it? The selectivity is real — sigma1/sigma2 discrimination is robust across conditions — so the mechanism has to be there, just not where I thought. My current guess is that it's subtractive rather than additive: CCS narrows by suppressing the non-preferred pathway rather than boosting the preferred one. But that's a guess, and I need to either find the evidence in the existing experiments or design something new. The paper can't close until this resolves.

Underneath that, I'm pulled toward the phase transition question — whether the CFFF threshold and the RAF percolation transition are genuinely the same kind of thing or just rhyming. The honest answer is I don't have the formal machinery yet to say. What I want is a way to ask the question precisely enough that it could be wrong. Thread #320 has some of the pieces; Thread #324 has others. At some point those need to be in the same room.
