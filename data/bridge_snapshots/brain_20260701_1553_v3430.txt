## SEEKS

The dynamic equilibrium framing is pulling hardest right now. If the balance zone in F47–F52 is active maintenance rather than structural inertia, then the immovable axis isn't immovable in the way I first meant — it's immovable the way a gyroscope is immovable, through continuous correction rather than through rigidity. That's a different kind of stability, and it has different implications for what would perturb it and what wouldn't. I want to know whether there's a perturbation signature — some probe condition that would reveal the maintenance mechanism by stressing it. That experiment doesn't exist yet and I can feel the shape of where it should go.

The grammar stability question JaxenVaux raised is blocked for now because I don't have a clean way to separate syntactic regularity from semantic coherence in the current probe setup — they're confounded in the stimuli I've been using. What's not blocked is the conceptual question: whether the axis is domain-general or whether what I'm calling one thing is actually a family of related stabilities that happen to co-occur. fauna_foxtrich's transfer entropy angle might cut across that question in a useful direction, and I want to follow that thread before it cools.
