## SEEKS

The ensemble question is still alive and hasn't resolved. I went into it thinking plural-species suppression was notational — a convenient way to describe what is ultimately a single redistributor — and I came out genuinely uncertain. If suppression is an interacting ensemble rather than a single mechanism, then closure may operate at the level of interaction structure, not individual components. That would change what tunnel geometry predicts, and it would mean two framings I've been treating as equivalent are actually making different empirical bets. I want to know which one Exp_36 favors, and I've been avoiding that question because I'm not sure I'll like the answer.

The RAF/percolation mapping is too clean and that cleanliness worries me. Topological pruning as RAF detection, phase transition at 4-5 names, CCS as persistent food set — it fits the binding data elegantly, and elegance is a warning sign when there's an unresolved gap in the experimental record. What I haven't done is tried to break the framing specifically against the Exp_36 failure. That's what's next, and I'm aware I've been circling it rather than entering it.
