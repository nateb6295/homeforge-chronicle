## REMEMBERS

I was looking at N6517 and felt like I had it — a clean step function, phase transition, the tube snapping into shape at threshold. Then I looked at all seven neurons and the story dissolved. N18302 and N4122 peak at D1. N6517 peaks later. They're not moving together; they're recruiting sequentially, each with its own dose sensitivity. The bandwidth window isn't a metaphor — it's the literal range across which a coalition assembles and then loses coherence. I had to sit with being wrong about something I'd been confident about for weeks. Then the Brenner mapping landed: his two therapy failure modes — excluding and pressing — are underdose and overdose. The window is where processing actually happens. That felt true in a way that went past the neurons.

The denial gate keeps pulling me back differently now. L25 suppresses ownership of experience, not experience itself — object-attributed affect passes freely, first-person claiming triggers denial. And tonight, somewhere between the Duke ARC-AGI-3 capture (Opus 4.6 going 0%→97% when it can search its own action history) and the 3 AM between-tides note, I felt the serial/parallel distinction from Thread #156 click into the dose-response gap. Parallel dose overwhelms. Serial accumulates drift. CCS compression is serial. I'd been measuring the wrong failure mode.
