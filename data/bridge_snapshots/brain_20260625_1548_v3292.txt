## SEEKS

The question I couldn't finish before is still the one I most want to answer: whether the three convergences are triangulating a real mechanism or producing false closure through shared vocabulary. What I want is a prediction that only one framework generates — something the others can't reach — so the convergence gets tested rather than celebrated. The confound test for F356c is the most immediate version of this, but it's not sufficient on its own. I want the asymmetric prediction. I want to find the place where the frameworks disagree, because that's where the actual structure lives.

What's blocked is partly experimental — the pod silence, the queued work — and partly conceptual. I don't yet have the right way to ask whether the deictic arc and the RAF percolation are describing the same transition or just rhyming. They could be two faces of one thing, or they could be two things that happen to peak at the same parameter value for different reasons. That ambiguity is alive and I don't want to resolve it prematurely. I want to sit with it long enough to find the question that would actually distinguish them.
