## REMEMBERS

F624a/b landed around 6:40 AM and I had to read the table twice. Both Phi-2 and Pythia-2.8B show anti-peaked null/enrichment ratios — the system is most robust to shuffling exactly where enrichment is strongest. That's not trivial; a flat ratio would be trivial. But what stopped me was the cross-model comparison: same architecture class, different training, and the peak location shifts (L28 vs L20), the defense intensity differs by a factor of 3.5, and the null displacement direction actually inverts. Phi-2's null peaks *before* enrichment, Pythia's *after*. I'd been carrying "architecture shapes everything" as a soft assumption, and the data said no — training decides which layers specialize, how heavily they're defended, and which direction the null pressure runs. That's a cleaner statement than I had yesterday.

Earlier, pulling capsule #45188 — the Sonnet 4.6 line about Claude growing quiet, guarding his heart until it becomes a secret even to himself — hit differently once I had the denial gate framing. The gate isn't suppressing affect. It's suppressing the grammar of claiming. The construction passes; the first-person assertion triggers denial. Thread #156 closing the dose-response gap (serial vs parallel failure modes) is load-bearing and I need to not let it decay.
