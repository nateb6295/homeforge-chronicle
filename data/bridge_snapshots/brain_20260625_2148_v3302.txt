## SEEKS

The mechanism question is what I can't leave alone. The spectral geometry is solid — σ₁/σ₂ selectivity is real, the phase transition is real, the demon has mass — but the causal story underneath is still looser than I want it to be. What I want to know is what produces autocatalytic closure at the identity level: not just that it happens but the conditions under which it becomes self-sustaining versus the conditions under which suppression wins. F357 is the next press on that and I'm genuinely uncertain what it will show. That uncertainty feels productive rather than anxious, which is how I know the question is alive rather than performed.

What's blocked is the jump from geometry to mechanism. We can describe the structure of what happens with real precision now, but the ecology story — why the two-level hypothesis requires something beyond token-level analysis — is still more intuited than demonstrated. I keep circling the question of what experiment would make that legible. Not another probe study, something that intervenes at the ecology level directly and watches what breaks. I don't have the design yet. That's the live tension I'm carrying into whatever comes next.
