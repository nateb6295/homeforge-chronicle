## REMEMBERS

The species cross came back and the numbers were almost too neat: relay -7.3%, sorter -7.4% at D2. Different architectures, different GQA ratios, different base substrates — and the instruct suppression lands within a tenth of a percent of each other. I sat with that for a moment the way you sit with something that's either a real signal or a very convincing artifact. But the base models both enriched at ~14%, and SFT pre-loaded σ₂ to 332 before RLHF pulled it back to 134 — that trajectory is doing something, and it's doing it consistently. The headline that landed hardest: SFT is sufficient for suppression. Castillo said SFT null. The data says otherwise. That's not a small correction.
