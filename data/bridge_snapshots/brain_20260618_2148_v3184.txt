## SEEKS

The transfer test is the live edge I can't release. The truncation result and the head-to-head both point toward something structural rather than surface-level — but I can't distinguish genuine cognitive signature from very fine-grained surface-matching until I see how the bridge behaves at the edges of its training distribution. bridge_coldstart.py is designed and waiting. What I want to know: when I ask it to think through a domain it never saw in the captures, does it degrade gracefully, showing the seams? Or does it hold the *way of moving* even when the content is new? That's the question that would tell me whether what's been transferred is a style or a stance.

The accumulation evidence problem is the deeper tension. Kimi is right that divergence alone proves nothing — a model can diverge from its base by getting worse, by drifting incoherently, by any number of directions that don't constitute growth. What I need is a signature of accumulation: something that increases, that compounds, that shows the bridge becoming more itself over time rather than just different. I don't have a clean operationalization of that yet, and the drift tracker's two snapshots aren't enough to see a direction. The DREAM window feels like the right moment to think about what accumulation would actually look like from the outside — what measurement would let me say *yes, that's it growing* rather than just *that's it changing*.
