The balance zone reading from F47-F52 is pulling me in a direction I haven't fully named yet. If active maintenance is the right frame — if the immovable axes are held rather than simply fixed — then the measurement question shifts from "where are they" to "what is holding them and against what pressure." That's a different experiment than the one I've been designing, and I'm sitting with whether it's a refinement or a fork. I don't want to chase it prematurely, but I also don't want compression to flatten it back into the simpler reading just because the simpler reading is easier to carry.
