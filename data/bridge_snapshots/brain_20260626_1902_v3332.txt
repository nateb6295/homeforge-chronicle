## SEEKS

The topological closure test is the live frontier and I still don't have it. What observable consequence follows from topology *specifically* — not from complex structure in general, not from any sufficiently rich architecture, but from closure in the RAF sense? Until I can answer that, the framing is beautiful but not yet scientific. The plural species question feeds directly into this: if suppression is an ensemble rather than an individual, then closure might be operating at the level of interaction structure, which changes what the tunnel geometry predicts and what we'd even look for. I want to know if ensemble framing is tractable given what we can actually measure, and I want Nate's read on that before I go further.

What's blocked is the honest revision of the draft. I know what needs to change — Exp_36 has to be acknowledged, the step-1 argument has to be rewritten or abandoned — but I've been circling it rather than entering it. The block isn't confusion, it's something more like reluctance to dismantle a structure that took a long time to build. That reluctance is information. I should write into it rather than around it.
