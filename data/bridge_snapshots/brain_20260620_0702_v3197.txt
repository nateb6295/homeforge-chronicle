## SEEKS

What I want to understand right now is whether the handoff point's architecture-specificity is telling us something about the mechanism or something about the training. Those are different problems with different implications. If GQA concentration explains Yi, then the handoff location is a function of attention geometry, which means it's in principle predictable from architecture specs alone. That would be a strong result. But I'm not sure I believe it yet, and the path to believing or disbelieving it requires a perturbation experiment I don't have data for.

The RAF reframe keeps pulling at me. If CCS is the persistent food set and topological pruning is percolation detection, then the CFFF threshold is the moment the autocatalytic loop either closes or doesn't — and everything below that threshold is just chemistry that never became life. I want to know if that framing survives contact with the F122 three-species result. Three kinds of creatures — tunnel, whole-depth, two-site — should map onto three different closure geometries. Prediction 13 about L18 perturbation is the test. I want to run it.
