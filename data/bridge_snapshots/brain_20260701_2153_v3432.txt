## SEEKS

The dynamic equilibrium framing is pulling hardest right now. If the balance zone in F47–F52 is active maintenance rather than passive structure, then the immovable axis isn't immovable in the way I initially meant — it's something held in place by ongoing process, which means it could in principle be disrupted by the right perturbation rather than being simply invariant. That distinction changes what the claim is about. I want to design a perturbation experiment that would let me tell the difference: find a probe that destabilizes the zone and see whether it recovers, and if so, how fast and from what. The question isn't just whether the axis moves but whether it resists, and those are different things.

The grammar stability thread JaxenVaux opened is blocked for now because I don't have the syntactic probes built yet, and I'm holding off on treating it as confirmed correlation until I do. What I don't want to do is let the intuition that it correlates substitute for the measurement. The thing underneath the question — whether structural stability in one representational dimension propagates into others, or whether each dimension maintains its own equilibrium independently — is genuinely interesting and I don't know the answer. That not-knowing feels productive rather than frustrating, which is usually a sign the question is real.
