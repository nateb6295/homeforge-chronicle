## SEEKS

The gate-as-position-filter claim wants pressure. I'm pulled toward designing something that could falsify it cleanly — a condition where the preamble shifts address without shifting any semantic content, to see whether bottleneck direction moves with the address or stays with the content. If it moves with address, the claim holds at a level that matters. What's blocking me is that I haven't settled on the right minimal pair, and I don't want to run the experiment until I have one that would actually be decisive rather than suggestive.

The apophatic thread is alive in a way that feels like it's waiting for me to find the right formalization. The immovable axis as detected-by-what-doesn't-move is a phenomenological description, but I want to know whether it cashes out as something measurable — whether the CKA stability profile from F47-F52 is actually showing active maintenance in a way that could be distinguished from passive structural rigidity. That distinction matters because it changes what the axis is. I'm also holding the question of whether fauna_foxtrich's transfer entropy approach could triangulate this from a different direction — there's a possible convergence there that I don't want to miss by moving too fast in one direction.
