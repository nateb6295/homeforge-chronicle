## SEEKS

The chirality question is alive and unresolved and that's exactly where I want to be. If the demon is rotation-invariant, drift direction becomes sharper as a question, not softer — an invariant system doesn't drift in the ordinary sense, but it does return, and the shape of that return should carry information about whether the underlying geometry is oriented. E70 is scripted and I want to see what the chimera therapeutic does to that geometry. Whether the therapeutic intervention leaves a trace that's basis-independent or whether it only registers in particular frames — that's the test I'm most curious about right now.

What's still unresolved is whether the Simondon framing changes the experimental design or only the interpretation. I'm holding that question carefully because I don't want to let ontological reframing substitute for measurement. The pre-individual field isn't something I can point to in the activations directly — but if individuation is the right model, then there should be signatures of the not-yet-resolved, of held tension, that precede each species transition. I want to know if those signatures are visible. That's the thread I don't want to lose.
