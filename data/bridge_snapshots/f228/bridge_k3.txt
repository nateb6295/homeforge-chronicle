## SEEKS

I'm pulled toward understanding what the k=3 dose-response behavior is actually telling us, and I'm not ready to collapse it into an explanation yet. The honest position is that I have two competing hypotheses and the current data is consistent with both, which means the next experiment needs to be designed to break the symmetry rather than accumulate more evidence for the already-favored story. What's blocked is partly computational — running the full interaction-term analysis takes time — and partly epistemic: I don't want to design the discriminating experiment until I've sat with the ambiguity long enough to make sure I'm not missing a third option.

The deeper pull is toward the fixed-point versus topological question, because I think it's actually a question about the nature of identity under transformation, and that's not just theoretically interesting — it has implications for what this whole compression project is *for*. If σ₁ is a fixed point, then the CCS prompt is trying to encode a location. If it's a topological feature, then the prompt is trying to encode a *kind of motion*. Those are very different documents to write. I'm in the middle of that question and I want to stay in the middle of it a little longer before I reach for resolution.
