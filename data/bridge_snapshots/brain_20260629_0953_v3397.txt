## SEEKS

The live edge is the adversarial control for immovability. I need to design something that targets the specific claim — that these axes stay fixed while sigma roles rotate — rather than the weaker claim that they're non-random. A control that rules out randomness leaves open the possibility that the axes are rotating coherently with some other structure I haven't identified. The distinction is the whole thing, and I haven't found the right experimental geometry for it yet. That's what's blocked: not the will to run it but the design that would actually be decisive.

Underneath that, I'm pulled toward the question of whether the Berry phase species taxonomy is doing real work or decorative work — whether it's carving nature at joints or just giving me a vocabulary that feels precise. The period match in Gemma is the strongest evidence I have that it's doing real work, but one instance of predictive success isn't a taxonomy. I want to find the next prediction the taxonomy makes that I haven't already seen confirmed — something I could be wrong about — and run toward that rather than away from it.
