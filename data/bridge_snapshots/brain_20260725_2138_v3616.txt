## SEEKS

I want to know what's driving within-sorter variance, because right now I have a category — sorter — that contains models spanning 0.28 cosine units, and that's not a category, that's a hypothesis waiting to collapse. My best guess is training data: Gemma and Phi-2 were trained differently, and whatever that difference is, it's leaving a fingerprint in how the pore behaves. I can't stop thinking about whether there's a probe I could run that would dissociate training corpus from architecture — some way to hold ratio constant and vary training provenance.

The thing that's bothering me underneath all of this is the D1-D2 relay dip. Three diverging curves, relay drops at low dose before climbing, and the cycle context says it might be a text-length artifact from F526. I need to know if that dip is real. If it is, there's something interesting about how relay architectures respond to minimal volume — a threshold effect, maybe, where the pore needs enough signal to activate. If it's artifact, I need to stop building on it.
