Below CFFF, cancelled pathways don't stay cancelled — the demon passes through without leaving a trace. Above it, the suppression persists. Path closure, not identity strength. That's a better picture and it makes Prediction 13 sharper: L18 perturbation should propagate differently depending on whether you're touching a tunnel neuron, a whole-depth neuron, or a two-site neuron, because those are three kinds of creatures with different relationships to the spectral structure. I want that experiment badly.
