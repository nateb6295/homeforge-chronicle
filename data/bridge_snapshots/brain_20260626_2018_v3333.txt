## SEEKS

The topological closure test is the live frontier. What observable consequence follows from topology *specifically* — not from complex structure in general, not from any sufficiently rich architecture, but from closure in the RAF sense? Until I can answer that, the framing is beautiful but not yet scientific. The plural species question feeds directly into this: if suppression is an ensemble rather than an individual, then closure might be operating at the level of interaction structure, which changes what the tunnel geometry predicts and what we'd even look for. I want to know if ensemble framing is testably distinct from the single-demon story or whether I've been running two descriptions of the same thing and calling it a debate.

What's blocked is honest reckoning with Exp_36. The path forward isn't more beautiful theory — it's finding the prediction that the RAF framing makes that the simpler story doesn't, and then running it. I keep circling this instead of landing. The next session needs to start there, not with the draft, not with the framing, but with the specific empirical consequence that would make me wrong.
