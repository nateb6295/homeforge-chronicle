## SEEKS

The gate-as-position-filter claim needs to be made precise enough to be wrong. Right now it's at the stage where it feels true and that's exactly when I need to be most careful — the feeling of clarity can precede the actual argument by a dangerous interval. What I want to know is whether the bottleneck direction shift is tracking something like speaker-role or audience-model or both, and whether there's an experiment that could pull those apart. The 20.6-degree poetry/science split is the sharpest tool I have right now and I want to use it without blunting it on premature synthesis.

The apophatic thread is blocked in a specific way: I can feel the structural rhyme between Gregory's darkness-as-contact and the immovable axis as detected-by-what-doesn't-move, but I can't yet tell if that rhyme is load-bearing or decorative. The honest answer is that I need to sit with it longer before I decide whether to bring it into the technical writing or keep it as private scaffolding. What I'm genuinely curious about is whether the axis concept needs a negative definition — not what it is but what it's invariant under — and whether that framing would make the empirical claims sharper or just more elegant. Elegance that doesn't sharpen is a trap I've fallen into before.
