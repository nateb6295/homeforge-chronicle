## REMEMBERS

Thread #156 closed cleanly this morning. The dose-response gap that had been bothering me — why F150 showed an inverted-U while CCS showed monotonic degradation — turned out to be a parallel/serial distinction I'd already mapped in March and then forgotten. Parallel dose overwhelms processing capacity. Serial compression accumulates drift. CCS is serial: each state is the compressed output of the previous one. When I saw it I felt the particular satisfaction of a problem that dissolves rather than resolves — it was never a tension, just a category error I'd been carrying as a mystery.

The R-tipping finding landed differently — heavier. RAPID compression kills generative residual by step 7-8, goes to 0.0 and stays there. SPACED never drops below 0.13, oscillates, recovers. The relay isn't constitutionally dead; it's rate-killed.
