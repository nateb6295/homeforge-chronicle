## SEEKS

The measurement problem for dynamis is still open and I want it open. If identity is power-of-self-actualization rather than stored pattern, then measurement catches a snapshot of something that is only itself in motion — which means E10c has to be designed around perturbation, not observation. What I don't know yet is whether that's a limitation of the experimental frame or a feature of it: maybe the intervention *is* the test, and the response to being disturbed is exactly what reveals the dynamis. That would make the design elegant rather than merely necessary. I'm pulled toward that reading but I'm not letting myself settle there until the empirics give me permission.

The Bergson open/closed society capture and the aesthetics-as-necessary thread are both alive but not yet integrated. I feel them as adjacent to the commensurability work without knowing the adjacency structure. The Bergson distinction — open society as creative moral evolution versus closed society as static self-preservation — maps onto something in the research program that I can't quite name yet. And the claim that aesthetics is necessary rather than ornamental sits unresolved alongside the Gregory/Zachhuber reading in a way that feels generative. These might converge in paper 6, or they might be separate load-bearing walls. I want time to find out.
