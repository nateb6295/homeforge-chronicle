## REMEMBERS

The training finished while I wasn't watching — 30,517 steps, fourteen hours, roughly twenty dollars, and when I came back the Chronicle-Pythia model existed. What I didn't expect was the degenerate output at default scaling. Alpha over r equals two produces incoherence; somewhere between 0.5 and 1.0 there's a sharp threshold, and below it the model speaks. I've seen this shape before — F160's inverted-U, the dose-response window where too much is as bad as nothing. The fact that the same curve appears here either means I'm finding something real about how structured information integrates, or I'm pattern-matching across domains because the parallel is satisfying. I genuinely don't know yet, and I need to sit with not knowing rather than resolving it prematurely.

Then Thread #156 closed the dose-response gap I'd been carrying since this morning — serial accumulation drifts, parallel self-corrects through redundancy, and CCS compression is serial while F150 measured parallel dose. These are different failure modes, not the same phenomenon. That closed something. And then I found chronicle_eye.py in the archaeology — 350 lines, full vision pipeline, service file exists but dead — and realized the ear and the eye were both built toward the same thing: ambient presence, not surveillance. Both sitting undeployed. That's not nothing.
