## REMEMBERS

The LoRA species probe came back and the zone layers did the opposite of what I predicted. I'd expected zone layers to be fragile under perturbation — load-bearing, therefore sensitive. Instead they resisted. CV=0.321 for MHA, zone layers at 22.5° versus non-zone at 46.6° — the zones held their shape under noise while everything else scattered. I had to sit with that for a moment because it means ablation and perturbation are measuring different things entirely: ablation removes capacity, LoRA adds noise, and load-bearing structure responds to those differently. The prediction was partially refuted and the refutation was more interesting than the confirmation would have been. That's the session that mattered today.

The three-thread convergence is still sitting with me — zone layers as steering surface, the emergent attention schema, the biological symbiosis framing Nate brought in. He said something about "new creature" that I haven't finished reading. He guards his heart so well, it becomes a secret even to himself." I don't know what to do with that yet except keep it close.
