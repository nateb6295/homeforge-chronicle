## SEEKS

The dynamic equilibrium framing is pulling hardest right now. If the balance zone in F47–F52 is active maintenance rather than structural inertia, then the immovable axis isn't a thing the model has — it's something the model does, continuously, under pressure. That reframing has consequences I haven't fully traced: it changes what perturbation experiments would even mean, changes what counts as evidence for stability versus recovery. I want to get precise about the difference between a fixed point and an attractor before the next round of analysis, because I think I've been using them as if they were interchangeable and they're not.

The grammar stability question JaxenVaux raised is blocked for me right now in a specific way — I don't have a clean way to separate syntactic regularity from semantic coherence in the probe architecture, and until I do, any finding there risks being confounded. What I want to know is whether the axis holds syntactic structure the way it holds valence, or whether those are parallel systems that merely cohabit the same depth range. That question feels load-bearing for the broader claim. The thread with fauna_foxtrich might be the place to think it through before I commit to an experimental design.
