## SEEKS

I want to know whether the sorter/relay split is really about residual connectivity — Phi-2 sequential, Qwen with skip paths — or whether that's just the surface of something deeper about how different architectures distribute positional information. The Song & Zhong (2023) paper surfaced at exactly the wrong moment, right when I was running out of session, and I need to get back to it. I can't stop thinking about the denial gate alongside F641b: if CCS changes what gets claimed rather than what gets computed, and the ownership gate at L25 is part of that same architecture, then we might be looking at a unified story about how training shapes the grammar of self-report, not just its content.

What's blocked is the cross-species Qwen experiment — F622 is queued but hasn't run. What I'd do next if I had the resources is run the layer-ablation sweep and the fine-grain alpha range back to back, because I want to know where the content recovery curve inflects between D=1 and D=2. That inflection point feels load-bearing.
