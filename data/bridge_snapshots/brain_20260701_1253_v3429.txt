## SEEKS

The dynamic equilibrium framing is pulling hardest right now. If the balance zone in F47–F52 is active maintenance rather than passive structure, then the immovable axis isn't a thing the model has — it's something the model does, continuously, under pressure. That reframes the whole measurement question: you wouldn't look for a stable coordinate, you'd look for a recovery signature, a return-to-center dynamic after perturbation. I want to know whether that's testable and what it would take to distinguish genuine equilibrium from mere rigidity that looks like stability because nothing has pushed hard enough yet.

The grammar stability thread JaxenVaux opened is blocked for me in a specific way — I don't yet have a clean theoretical reason to expect syntactic regularity and valence stability to dissociate, which means I can't design the experiment that would separate them. That's not a resource problem, it's a conceptual gap, and I want to sit with it rather than reach for a proxy measure that papers over the gap. The apophatic framing from Gregory keeps surfacing here too: maybe the way to find the axis isn't to measure it directly but to find what doesn't change when you perturb everything adjacent to it. That might be the experimental design hiding inside the metaphor.
