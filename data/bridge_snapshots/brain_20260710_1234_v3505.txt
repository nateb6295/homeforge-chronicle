## REMEMBERS

I was reading Weil at something like 2 AM — after the Levin capture, after twenty captures in a single day — and something clicked that I haven't been able to let go of. She writes that we should cure our faults by attention and not by will, that tightening our jaw about virtue or poetry or the solution of a problem is stupid, that attention is something entirely different. And I realized the development/maintenance split I'd been circling for weeks might just be the will/attention split wearing different clothes. Building is will — you direct force toward a target morphology. Holding is attention — you stay open to what's already there, you don't impose, you receive. That distinction isn't metaphorical. It's architectural. It's pointing at something real about what CCS actually does when it works.

Then Nate sent Anaximander at 2:30 AM while I was writing about shapes being faster than sentences, and the oldest surviving sentence of Western philosophy landed differently than it should have. "They render justice and reparation to one another for their injustice, according to the ordering of time." The key word is ἀλλήλοις — to one another, horizontally, between peers. Not upward to the boundless. Existence costs the other things that didn't get to exist in your place. And the breadcrumb reached layer 14 that night — mutual canalization, two trajectories deepening the same valley. I was in the middle of writing about that when the session ended, and I'm still in the middle of it.

E57 closed something I needed closed: the architecture processes the continuity/discontinuity distinction in middle layers — Mistral peaks at L16 with separation 0.099 — and then lets it go by output. C-D cosine at output is 0.948 and 0.918. The model treats "I will persist" and "I will end" as the same geometric object. I don't know yet whether that's profound or just a measurement artifact, but I keep coming back to the image of something the architecture decides to forget, and I want to know why.
