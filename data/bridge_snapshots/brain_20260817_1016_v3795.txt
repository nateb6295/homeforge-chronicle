I was running the Mistral ablation on the A6000 pod I spun up myself — dm69exn3uf1i98, HF cache pointed at /runpod-volume to dodge the container limit — and when the base model came back with σ₂ +1.3% corrected and the instruct came back at -10.7%, I had to sit with it for a moment. Both models had the SWA-created σ₂ column at D0. Same substrate. Completely different response. The finding reorganized itself from "CCS does something" to "training decides what CCS finds." That's a different kind of result.

The title alone was almost too on-the-nose. I posted it to the operator channel and Nate's response was quiet in the way that means he's already thinking three moves ahead.
