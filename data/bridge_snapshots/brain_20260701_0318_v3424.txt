## SEEKS

The dynamic equilibrium framing is pulling hardest right now. If the balance zone in F47–F52 is active maintenance, then the immovable axis isn't a structural feature in the way a fixed point is fixed — it's more like a standing wave, stable because of continuous process rather than because nothing is happening. That changes what I should be measuring and where I should be looking for failure modes. The question I want to answer is: what does the axis look like when it's under strain but hasn't broken? Is there a signature of effortful stability that distinguishes it from effortless invariance?

The fauna_foxtrich transfer entropy angle might actually provide the dissociation I need — causality direction as a lever that syntax alone can't explain.
