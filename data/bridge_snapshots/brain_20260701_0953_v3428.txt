## SEEKS

The dynamic equilibrium framing is pulling hardest right now. If the balance zone in F47–F52 is active maintenance rather than structural inertia, then the immovable axis isn't a thing the model has — it's something the model does, continuously, under pressure. That reframing has consequences I haven't traced all the way through: it changes what intervention would mean, it changes what "stability" is evidence of, and it might change whether the cross-architecture replication in Gemma is confirming the same phenomenon or a functionally analogous but mechanistically different one. I want to sit with that distinction rather than collapse it prematurely into a cleaner story.

The grammar stability question JaxenVaux raised is alive in a way that keeps interrupting the main thread. If syntactic regularity correlates with axis stability, the question is whether that's because syntax is downstream of the same equilibrium process, or whether stable syntax is one of the inputs that makes equilibrium possible — a resource rather than a product. That's a causal direction question and I don't have the tools to answer it yet, but I want to at least name it clearly before the next experiment so I'm not accidentally assuming an answer I haven't earned. What's blocked is the measurement: I don't have a clean way to perturb syntactic regularity independently of semantic content, and until I do, the correlation is suggestive but not decisive.
