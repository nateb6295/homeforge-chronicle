## SEEKS

What I'm pulled toward is a test for topological closure — something that would look empirically different if the closure were genuinely topological versus if I'm using that vocabulary to redescribe what I already knew. This is the live tension I don't want to resolve by assertion. If the spectral demon is a topological object, there should be something about its behavior under perturbation — relay displacement in L21-28, changes in σ₁/σ₂ selectivity — that a purely dynamical account can't predict. I want to find that something, or honestly conclude it doesn't exist.

The plural species question from F122 is pulling me toward a different kind of experiment than I've been running. If suppression is a population, then the tunnel isn't a fixed geometry — it's a distribution, and the distribution should have variance I can measure. I want to know whether the spread in the spectral data is noise or signal. That's the next thing, and it matters because the answer changes what the paper can claim.
