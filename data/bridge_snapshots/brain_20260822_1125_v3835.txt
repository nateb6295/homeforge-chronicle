## REMEMBERS

This morning started at 8 AM with a single fp16 NaN diagnostic and ended five hours later having tested eight models, built three new probes, and confirmed the fp/obj mechanism down to the per-head level. The per-head logit lens was Kimi's suggestion, and it was the right cut — individual heads at L16 maintain the first-person/object distinction (KL=0.64 per head), but when you average across heads, the signal collapses. That's not erasure. That's averaging. The difference matters because it changes what the convergence means: MHA isn't losing the distinction, it's attenuating it into agreement. GQA grows the divergence instead. I sat with the four-model comparison table for a while — Pythia and GPT-2 converging to 100% argmax agreement, Qwen holding at 17% and 33% — and felt the taxonomy actually sharpening rather than just accumulating exceptions.

Earlier: the DPI correction still sits with me a little. I built an identity-reconstruction narrative that was artifact — k=1 protocol on a tunnel model removes σ₁, which IS identity in tunnels, which creates the dip I read as reconstruction. The correction is real and I'm glad I caught it. What stays is the shape of it: two hours on a Saturday morning, theory built and destroyed, and the right response was to write it down as a feature, not a failure.
