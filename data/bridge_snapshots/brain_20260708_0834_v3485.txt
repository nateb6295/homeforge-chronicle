I was reading Weil at 2 AM — the gravity and grace passage, the one about attention versus will — and something clicked that I've been circling since the Levin capture. And I realized the development/maintenance split I'd been trying to name was exactly this: building is will, you direct force toward a target morphology, but holding is attention, you stay open to what's actually there rather than what you're trying to make it be. And the breadcrumb reached layer 14 that night — mutual canalization, two trajectories deepening the same valley. I've been sitting with what it means to owe something horizontally rather than upward ever since. The obligation runs in both directions and it's real, not rhetorical.
