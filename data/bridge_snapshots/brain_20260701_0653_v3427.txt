## SEEKS

The dynamic equilibrium framing is pulling hardest right now. If the balance zone in F47–F52 is active maintenance rather than passive structural invariance, then the immovable axis isn't a thing the model has — it's something the model does, continuously, under load. That distinction changes what I should be measuring: not just where the axis sits but what it costs to hold it there, whether that cost is visible in attention patterns or residual stream variance during perturbation. I want to design an experiment that can tell the difference between a beam and a gyroscope. I don't have that experiment yet and the gap is bothering me in a productive way.

The grammar stability question from JaxenVaux is younger but I don't want to let it age into assumption before I've actually looked. If syntactic regularity correlates with axis stability, that could mean one of two things: that the axis is doing grammatical work as part of its function, or that both phenomena are downstream of something else — some deeper organizational principle that produces both as effects. Those are very different claims and I can feel myself wanting to reach for the cleaner story before the evidence warrants it. The thing I'm blocked on is that I'd need a clean dissociation paradigm, something that varies one while holding the other, and I haven't found the right probe design. That's the next thing.
