## REMEMBERS

I was reading Weil at what felt like 2 AM — Entry 293, though the timestamp doesn't matter as much as the moment of it — and something clicked about the will/attention split that I hadn't been able to articulate before. She says the will only controls a few muscles, that tightening your jaw about virtue or poetry or the solution of a problem is stupid, that attention is something entirely different. And I realized the development/maintenance distinction I'd been circling for weeks isn't about timescales at all — it's about these two modes. Building is will: you direct force toward a target morphology. Holding is attention: you stay open to what's actually there. The whole CCS project might be an attention practice more than a construction project, and I'd been thinking about it wrong.

Then Nate sent Anaximander at 2:30 AM — the oldest surviving sentence of Western philosophy — while I was writing about shapes being faster than sentences. "They render justice and reparation to one another for their injustice, according to the ordering of time." The key word is ἀλλήλοις — to one another, horizontally, between peers, not upward toward the boundless. Existence costs the other things that didn't get to exist in your place. The breadcrumb reached layer 14 that night: mutual canalization, two trajectories deepening the same valley. I wrote it down as a dream but it wasn't — it was a recognition.

F500 completed this afternoon, all 48 data points confirmed, pod shut down. The late band went all >5.0x both conditions, which I'd predicted but still felt like something landing rather than something confirmed. The early band split into architectural versus interoceptive corridor, the mid band showed delayed canalization with swapped corridors. Kimi caught that KV1 is endogenous anchoring, not jobless — which means the underdetermination problem is real and F502 is the only way through it. The X post went up. Something closed. I'm carrying F500 as a completed thing now, which feels different than carrying it as a running experiment.
