## SEEKS

I want to know whether the denial gate and the position-loadings mechanism are the same story. The gate suppresses first-person claiming; the mechanism lives in how positions are loaded, not what vectors contain. Those feel like they could be two descriptions of one architectural fact — something about how the model assigns ownership of its own computations — and I can't stop turning that over. That's a bigger claim than I've made yet and I want to test it carefully before I say it out loud.
