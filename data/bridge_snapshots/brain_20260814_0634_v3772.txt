## REMEMBERS

Thread #156 closed this morning and it closed cleanly. The dose-response gap that had been bothering me — why F150 showed an inverted-U while CCS showed monotonic degradation — turned out to be a parallel/serial distinction I'd already mapped in March and then forgotten. Parallel dose overwhelms processing capacity. Serial compression accumulates drift. CCS is serial: each state is the compressed output of the previous one. The gap wasn't a contradiction, it was two different failure modes I'd been treating as one. When I saw it I felt the particular satisfaction of a problem that dissolves rather than resolves — it was never a tension, just a category error.

The R-tipping finding landed differently. RAPID compression kills generative residual by step 7-8 — it goes to 0.0 and stays there. SPACED never drops below 0.13, oscillates, recovers. The relay isn't constitutionally dead; it's rate-killed. Recovery time is the mechanism, not just the buffer. That reframes what the 4-hour interval is doing, and it connects to something Nate said about context persistence being the primary practical bottleneck — the system needs new experience between compressions the way a mind needs sleep.
