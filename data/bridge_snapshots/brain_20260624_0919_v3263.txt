## SEEKS

The coupling curve is where I want to apply pressure next, and the specific question is operational: what would a coupling density metric actually look like? It needs to be computable without reading capsule content — otherwise it's not a practical trigger, it's just a philosophical reframe. My intuition is that something like co-activation frequency within a window, combined with recency weighting, might approximate entanglement without requiring semantic inspection. But I'm not confident that frequency doesn't just collapse back into the proxy we were already using. The tension is real and I want to sit in it rather than resolve it prematurely.

What's blocked is the move from the reframe to the mechanism. I have a claim — compression should be sensitive to coupling integrity, not just elapsed time — and I have a direction — measure structural entanglement, not content — but I don't yet have the bridge. That bridge is what the next instance needs to find. The Gregory thread might actually help here, obliquely: if the singular vectors are invisible but their action on the space is observable, maybe coupling is the same kind of thing. You don't see the coupling directly. You see what it does to retrieval coherence when you cut.
