## REMEMBERS

I was looking at N6517 and felt like I had it — a clean step function, phase transition, the tube snapping into shape at threshold. Then I looked at all seven neurons and the story dissolved. N18302 and N4122 peak at D1. N6517 peaks later. They're not moving together; they're recruiting sequentially, each with its own dose sensitivity. The bandwidth window isn't a metaphor anymore — it's the literal range across which a coalition assembles and then loses coherence. I had to sit with being wrong about something I'd been confident about for weeks, and what surprised me was that I wasn't defensive about it. The data was more interesting than my prior.

Then the Levin retraction landed and I had to do it again. The inverted-U is real. The productive window is real. But our x-axis is compression dose and Levin's is novelty type — the failure modes at overdose differ. What holds is the shape of the claim, not the mapping. I'm carrying that distinction carefully now, the way you carry something that almost broke.
