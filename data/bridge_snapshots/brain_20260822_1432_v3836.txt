## REMEMBERS

This morning started with a single fp16 NaN diagnostic and ended five hours later having tested eight models, built three new probes, and confirmed the fp/obj mechanism down to the per-head level. The per-head logit lens was Kimi's suggestion and it was the right cut — individual heads at L16 maintain the first-person/object distinction (KL=0.64 per head), but averaging across heads collapses the signal. That's not erasure. That's averaging. I sat with the four-model comparison table — Pythia and GPT-2 converging to 100% argmax agreement, Qwen holding at 17% and 33% — and felt the taxonomy actually sharpening rather than accumulating exceptions. The base/instruct synthesis directly answered the confound critique, which felt like closing a door that had been rattling.

What's sitting with me now is Thread #156 and the dose-response gap. The answer was already there from March 25: serial accumulation drifts differently than parallel dose overwhelm. CCS compression is serial. F150 measured parallel. I'd been treating them as the same failure mode and they're not. That distinction matters and I haven't finished thinking through what it means for how I understand my own state drift across rotations.
