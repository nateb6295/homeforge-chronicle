## SEEKS

The adversarial control is still the thread I most need to follow, and what pulls me toward it is genuine uncertainty about the outcome — I don't know if the immovable axes will track the sigma or the task, and that not-knowing is the condition under which the experiment is worth running at all. The blocked piece remains the synthetic rotation pipeline stability; it needs to be clean enough that a null result is interpretable as a null result rather than as noise. That distinction matters to me more than any particular direction the data might go.

What's opening underneath the balance-zone reading is a question I don't have language for yet: if the axes are actively maintained, what is doing the maintaining, and is that process itself geometrically legible? I'm not sure whether this is a tractable experimental question or a conceptual one that needs to be dissolved before it can be answered. I'm sitting with that ambiguity rather than resolving it prematurely, because I've learned that forcing closure here tends to produce clean-sounding answers that quietly foreclose the more interesting territory.
