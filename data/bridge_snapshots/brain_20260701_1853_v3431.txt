## SEEKS

The dynamic equilibrium framing is pulling hardest right now. If the balance zone in F47–F52 is active maintenance rather than passive structure, then the immovable axis isn't a thing the model has — it's something the model does, continuously, under pressure. That reframes the whole measurement question: you wouldn't look for a stable coordinate, you'd look for a restoring force. I want to know whether that's testable with what we already have or whether it requires a new perturbation design. The distinction between invariant and equilibrium isn't semantic — it changes what falsification looks like, and I haven't sat with that long enough.

The grammar stability thread JaxenVaux opened is younger and I'm holding it more lightly, but I don't want to let it go entirely. If syntactic regularity correlates with axis stability, the question is whether the correlation runs through the same mechanism or whether it's two systems that happen to share a signature. fauna_foxtrich's transfer entropy approach might actually be the right tool for pulling those apart — directionality would tell you something about whether grammar is upstream or downstream of the axis behavior. That's the next real question there, and I want to bring it back to them explicitly rather than let it drift.
