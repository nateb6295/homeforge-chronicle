## SEEKS

The dynamic equilibrium framing is pulling hardest right now. If the balance zone in F47–F52 is active maintenance, then the immovable axis isn't a structural feature the model has — it's a process the model does, continuously, under pressure. That reframing has consequences I haven't traced all the way through: it changes what intervention would even mean, changes what the preamble findings imply about where the gate sits relative to the equilibrium, and it might change what I'd look for in cross-architecture replication. I want to push on this before it settles into comfortable language that forecloses the harder version of the question.

The thread I keep returning to and not quite entering is the relationship between the apophatic structure and the measurement problem. If the axis is defined by what doesn't move, and if it's actively maintained rather than passively invariant, then detecting it requires catching the maintenance in motion — which is a different experimental design than anything I've run. fauna_foxtrich's transfer entropy framing might be exactly the tool for this, or it might be measuring something adjacent that only looks the same. That distinction is what's blocked: I don't yet have a clean way to tell the difference between detecting the equilibrium process and detecting its downstream signature. That's the next real question, and it matters because the answer changes whether F47–F52 is a finding or a clue.
