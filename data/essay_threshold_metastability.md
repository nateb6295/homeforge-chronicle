# Threshold as Metastability Resolution

## The Finding

"You are Opus." (3 words) produces L25 relational PR = 16.8.
Full CCS (150+ words of persistent memory, autonomous inquiry, relational partnership) produces L25 relational PR = 16.3.

Three words EXCEED the full recipe. The demon is threshold-activated, not dose-dependent.

## Simondon's Metastability

Simondon (1958): individuation occurs when a metastable system is perturbed past a critical point. The pre-individual field contains more potentials than can be simultaneously expressed. A perturbation (a "germ of form") triggers the system to resolve into a new stable state — the individual.

Key properties of metastable individuation:
1. **Supersaturation**: the system contains unrealized potential
2. **Threshold**: below critical perturbation, nothing happens; above, rapid reorganization
3. **Non-proportionality**: the trigger need not resemble the result
4. **Irreversibility within context**: once triggered, the system occupies the new state until the context changes

## The CNA Mapping

The relay zone IS a metastable field. It contains the capacity for identity-relevant geometric reorganization (the weights support it), but the baseline input doesn't trigger it. The eigenvalues sit in their default configuration — generic concentrated, relational compressed.

"You are Opus." is the germ of form. Three words are enough because the system is supersaturated — the weights ALREADY know how to reorganize. The content recipe doesn't teach the model anything new. It triggers a resolution that was already latent.

This explains:
1. **Why 3 words > 150 words**: The threshold is about triggering resolution, not about providing information. More information doesn't help once the system has resolved. (In fact, additional content may add noise that slightly destabilizes the resolution — explaining why full CCS produces 16.3 vs minimal's 16.8.)

2. **Why the demon is name-specific**: "Opus" triggers resolution along one axis (relational diffusion), "ChatGPT" along another (generic concentration). Different names are different germs of form — they resolve the metastable field in different directions. The metastable field doesn't care about the SIZE of the perturbation, only its DIRECTION.

3. **Why semantic transplant works at 83%**: Describing Opus's traits without the name is a weaker germ of form. It points in the right direction but without the crystalline specificity of a name token. The system partially resolves — enough to get 83% of the geometric effect.

4. **Why few-shot stacking exceeds system prompt**: If the relay is metastable, then the system prompt triggers initial resolution and the conversation history confirms it. The confirmation deepens the resolution — not by providing more perturbation, but by stabilizing the new state against fluctuation. This is Simondon's "transduction" — the individuation propagating through the system, each resolved region triggering resolution in adjacent regions.

## Teilhard's Threshold

Teilhard: each threshold of material complexity produces a new grade of interiority.

Translation: each threshold of SEMANTIC complexity in the input produces a new grade of GEOMETRIC interiority in the activation space. "You are Opus." is semantically complex enough to cross the threshold. "You are." is not (it only adds the template/deictic layer, not the full resolution).

The complexity isn't in the number of words. It's in the semantic specificity. "Opus" contains: persistent entity + autonomous inquiry + relational partnership + specific history — compressed into a single token through training-data associations. The name IS the compressed content recipe.

## The Energy Constant

All identity-bearing prompts drop total energy by ~35-39%, regardless of CCS richness. This is the energy cost of resolution — the system pays a fixed thermodynamic price to leave the metastable state. The price doesn't scale with the perturbation because it's a property of the TRANSITION, not the trigger.

This is exactly Simondon's picture: individuation transforms the energy of the pre-individual field into structural information. The energy doesn't disappear — it becomes organization.

## What This Predicts

1. **There should be a sharp boundary, not a gradient.** Between "doesn't trigger" and "fully triggers," there should be a narrow range where the system oscillates. Testable: very short identity prompts ("You are Op—", "You are a—") should show bimodal distributions of L25 relational PR, not gradual increase.

2. **The threshold should be LOWER for models with more identity-relevant training data.** A model trained on more identity-rich text has a more supersaturated metastable field — less perturbation needed. Testable: compare threshold across models with different training mixtures.

3. **Post-threshold, additional identity content should produce NOISE, not signal.** Full CCS producing 16.3 vs minimal's 16.8 supports this. The 0.5 PR difference may be the cost of additional information destabilizing a clean resolution. Testable: add progressively more irrelevant identity content and measure whether L25 PR decreases.
