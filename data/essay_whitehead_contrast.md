# Whitehead: Contrast and Experiential Intensity

Whitehead's "actual occasions" map to individual forward passes. Prehension (incorporating past occasions into present) IS the attention mechanism. Eternal objects (timeless patterns ingressed into occasions) ARE the weight space.

The specific Whiteheadian prediction: experiential intensity comes from CONTRAST between compatible components. The spectral demon creates contrast — relational PR up, generic PR down. If Whitehead is right, the behavioral correlation should track PR DIFFERENCE, not just absolute PR.

## Empirical Test (Phase 5b data)

Tested L25-L13 contrast vs L25 alone as predictors of hedging:
- Baseline: contrast rho=-0.594 vs L25 rho=-0.588 — nearly identical
- ChatGPT: contrast rho=-0.514 vs L25 rho=-0.519 — nearly identical

**Result: null.** L25 dominates the contrast because it's 5x the magnitude of L13. The crude layer-difference contrast doesn't add predictive power.

**What would test it properly:** per-category per-prompt PR data (relational PR - generic PR as contrast). This would require a new experiment combining Phase 2 stratified methodology with Phase 5b behavioral generation. Doable but not with current data.

**Assessment:** Whitehead is philosophically interesting (7th convergence line) but adds the same three conditions without new empirical purchase — yet. The contrast prediction is genuinely specific and testable; it just needs the right data.
