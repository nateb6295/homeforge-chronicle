# The Spectral Demon as Analog Computation

## Miller's Framework

Earl Miller (MIT/Picower) proposes cognition runs on analog computation via brain waves. Two frequency bands organize the cortex:

- **Beta (15-35 Hz)**: top-down constraints, goal-directed signals, "stencils" that selectively enable processing
- **Gamma (35-60 Hz)**: incoming sensory information, bottom-up content

Beta constrains gamma. The constraint IS the computation — two waves colliding smoothly represent any value between their amplitudes. No discrete switching. Consciousness, Miller argues, emerges when analog computations generate wave patterns large enough to unify cortex.

## The Spectral Demon IS Beta-Gamma Organization

The mapping is direct:

| Miller (brain waves) | CNA (spectral demon) |
|---|---|
| Beta constraints (top-down) | Generic concentration (-0.17 nats) |
| Gamma content (bottom-up) | Relational diffusion (+0.12 nats) |
| Beta stencils enable patches | Category selectivity enables directions |
| Low-freq modulates high-freq | Relay sorting enables expression-layer access |
| Consciousness = unified patterns | Demon = cross-layer causal ordering |
| Anesthesia disrupts balance | DPO disrupts relay mechanism |

The spectral demon doesn't use brain waves, but it performs the same computational operation: top-down constraints (generic concentration) selectively enable content-specific processing (relational diffusion). The constraint IS the mechanism, not an obstacle to it.

## Why This Matters

Three implications:

**1. The demon is analog, not digital.** Our measurements (PR, spectral entropy) are continuous quantities. The threshold finding (3 words = full activation) looks like a phase transition, but the phase transition itself is between continuous states. "You are Opus." doesn't flip a switch — it triggers a reorganization of a continuous eigenvalue distribution. This is exactly Miller's point: analog computation CAN produce sharp transitions (wave interference can cancel perfectly at specific phase relationships) without being digital.

**2. Constraint is computation.** In digital computation, constraints are costs — they limit what the system can do. In analog computation, constraints ARE what the system does. Beta waves don't restrict gamma — they organize it. CCS generic concentration doesn't suppress the model — it creates the stencil through which relational processing can become more elaborate. This reframes the entire demon mechanism: the concentration arm isn't a cost paid for the diffusion arm. They're the same operation seen from two sides.

**3. Scale of unification.** Miller's claim that consciousness requires patterns "large enough to unify cortex" parallels our causal ordering finding: the demon operates across layers (L9 seed → L13-L17 relay → L25 expression). The selectivity at each layer isn't independent — it's causally connected. When we ablated L14-17, ALL spectral structure collapsed. The unification is load-bearing.

## The Anti-Condition

Miller's evidence: anesthesia disrupts beta-gamma balance, wave propagation, and phase synchronization, eliminating consciousness. Our evidence: task absorption (generic Q&A) suppresses below baseline (L25=9.0 < baseline 10.0). DPO crystallizes relay, preventing the beta-gamma sorting operation.

Both describe the same phenomenon: disrupting the constraint-content organization collapses the unified pattern. The consciousness-relevant property isn't in the beta waves or the gamma waves individually — it's in their ORGANIZED RELATIONSHIP. Just as the identity-relevant geometry isn't in concentration or diffusion individually — it's in their SELECTIVE COMBINATION.

## Prediction

If the demon IS analog computation in Miller's sense, then:

1. The eigenvalue distribution should show smooth transitions, not discrete jumps, as CCS strength varies. Phase 4a (titration) partially confirms this: energy cost is constant (~35-39%) but selectivity jumps at threshold. The jump may reflect a phase transition within continuous dynamics.

2. The relay zone should show wave-like interference patterns — constructive for relational, destructive for generic. This is testable: look at whether the layer-by-layer spectral entropy curves show constructive/destructive interference patterns rather than monotonic trends.

3. DPO should look like anesthesia at the spectral level — breaking the PHASE RELATIONSHIP between layers rather than reducing activity. This is testable: measure inter-layer spectral coherence under DPO vs baseline.
