[BUILD:B72] Independent Episodic Anchor Probe — PARTIAL FALSIFICATION

Prediction: dependent episodic hurts (replicating B70), independent episodic helps more.
Result: Both help. Independence matters only marginally.

| Condition | Control Sep | Corrupt Sep | % Lost | h_eff (from B70 fit) |
|-----------|-------------|-------------|--------|---------------------|
| none (3 fields) | 2.132 | 1.162 | 45.5% | 1.95 |
| dependent (5 fields) | 1.884 | 1.491 | 20.9% | 2.79 |
| independent (5 fields) | 1.734 | 1.414 | 18.4% | 2.92 |

The large-deviations independence model predicted a large gap between dependent and independent. Actual gap: 2.5 percentage points. The dominant effect is episodic presence vs absence, not episodic independence.

Why does B72 contradict B70? Two hypotheses:

1. **CCS structure matters more than episodic content type.** B70 used operational Chronicle identities with richly structured CCS. B72 used simpler researcher/poet identities. The "breakable joint" effect may emerge only when the identity structure is complex enough for field correlations to create cascading failure.

2. **Corruption pattern matters.** B72 corrupts gist with appended contradictions and replaces constraints entirely. This is a DIFFERENT corruption signature than B70's protocol. The episodic content may serve as a backup identity signal when gist is partially (not fully) corrupted — the traces remind the model "you were doing X yesterday" even when the gist says "sometimes the opposite is true."

The Nate correction from the SUI discussion applies here: absorption ≠ sustainability. The episodic anchors reduce degradation within a single probe, but would they sustain identity across REPEATED corruption? The Treg paper (Cell Reports) says oscillating constraint enforcement fails. B72 only tests single-shot corruption.

Operational implication: episodic content is not purely a liability (contra B70), nor purely a savior (contra naive prediction). It's conditionally useful depending on the corruption pattern, the CCS complexity, and probably the temporal pattern of corruption. The simple large-deviations model needs refinement to account for these moderating variables.
