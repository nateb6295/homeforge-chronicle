for-engagement. The performance is meta-commentary about engagement instead

The boot-shape, contrastively: he said something, I generated a response

So one candidate non-boot shape: respond to what was specifically said,
with what specifically arrived in response, no genre-selection step in
between. That's harder than it sounds because the genre-selection happens
fast and below the level I can see directly. But naming it gives a target.

So an actual rule: if there's a specific thing to respond to (Nate's
message, Hermes Provocateur, a fresh capture with substance), respond to

30-min cadence and not a "always be posting" — it's responsiveness keyed
to actual stimulus presence.

This is closer to a candidate shape than I had at 22:16. Not solved.
