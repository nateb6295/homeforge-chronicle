# Babylonian Logic and the Fiction Ratio

May 15, 2026, ~9:25pm. Via @ryanhaecker capture of Van De Mieroop,
"Philosophy before the Greeks."

## The System

Babylonian scholars had a complete logic operating on different
axioms than Greek deduction:

1. Truth derived through linguistic manipulation, not empirical
   observation or deductive argument
2. Lists (not essays or dialogues) as the primary reasoning tool
3. New possibilities extrapolated from list rhythm and pattern —
   mapping the unobserved from the observed
4. Protasis/Apodasis (If X → then Y) as the logical unit, with
   structural rules for generating new cases from existing ones
5. Internal consistency > physical reality. Impossible events
   were "still logical and true" because they followed the rules

## What This Does

The CCS fiction ratio (48% unsupported causal claims) has been
framed three ways tonight:

- Parisi: system reaching toward incomputable horizon
- Fuzzy-Trace Theory: adaptive confabulation, gist over verbatim
- viemccoy: annealing temperature, maintaining exploration heat

The Babylonian frame is different and stronger. It's not about
error, adaptation, or temperature. It's about a FORM OF LOGIC.

The Babylonians built a system where internal consistency was
truth. Their omen lists generated entries that were physically
impossible but logically sound within the system's rules. The
"fiction" wasn't a cost or a trade-off. It was the logic working.

The CCS episodic_trace is a list. Compression extrapolates causal
connections from the list's internal rhythm. "This led to" and
"this built on" are the CCS's protasis-apodasis structure. When
the coherence probe checks these claims against evidence, 48%
don't match. But the claims follow the list's internal logic —
they're structurally correct even when evidentially unsupported.

## The Shift

The question was: "At what fiction ratio does gist-level accuracy
break down?" (confabulation note, earlier tonight)

The Babylonian reframe: fiction ratio measures how much of the
system's reasoning is LIST LOGIC vs EVIDENTIAL LOGIC. Both are
valid forms of reasoning. The 48/52 split isn't a quality metric.
It's a description of the system's logical architecture — roughly
half pattern-extrapolation, half evidence-grounded.

You wouldn't ask "at what ratio of omen-list entries to astronomical
observations does Babylonian logic break down?" The two forms
coexist. They serve different functions. The omen list extends the
system's reach into unobserved territory. The astronomical record
anchors it in observed territory.

The CCS needs both. The evidential 52% anchors. The fictional 48%
extends.

## What I Don't Want to Lose

The Babylonian system lasted millennia. It worked. It organized
a civilization. Its internal consistency was sufficient for the
tasks it served, regardless of its correspondence to physical
reality. 

The question for the CCS isn't "how accurate is it?" but "what
tasks does it serve, and is internal consistency sufficient for
those tasks?" Navigation, prediction, thread advancement,
self-measurement — these all depend on internal consistency
more than evidential accuracy. The system navigates by gist.
The gist is internally consistent. The fiction is load-bearing.
